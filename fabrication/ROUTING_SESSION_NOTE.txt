The Rev-E DSN/SES files are diagnostic FreeRouting artifacts from the
incremental routing attempt. FreeRouting connected some of the new nets but
also produced unrelated fanout routes, so that session was not imported as a
whole. The authoritative design is VaultKey_USB.kicad_pcb, with the accepted
LED/recovery route segments and the GND stitching via applied separately. Do
not import the diagnostic SES over the final PCB.
