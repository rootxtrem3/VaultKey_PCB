The VaultKey_USB.dsn and VaultKey_USB.ses files are retained as historical
FreeRouting artifacts from the initial route. The authoritative final design
is VaultKey_USB.kicad_pcb, which includes subsequent DRC cleanup, GND island
stitching, corrected PCA9306 pin mapping, and the TI-recommended R8/C5 bias
network. Do not import the historical SES over the final PCB.
